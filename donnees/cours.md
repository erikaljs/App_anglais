## Mode d'emploi

Ce document contient les 20 cours du programme A1 → B2, un par module de 4 semaines. Le vocabulaire est dans le tableur fourni à part : 955 mots au total (A1 : 237, A2 : 233, B1 : 246, B2 : 239), rangés par niveau et par module, avec la catégorie de l'appli correspondante.

Chaque cours suit la même structure :

1. **Objectifs** : ce que tu sais faire à la fin du module
2. **Leçons** : la règle expliquée simplement, un tableau des formes et des exemples
3. **Pièges à éviter** : les erreurs typiques des francophones
4. **Exercices** : à faire sans regarder la leçon
5. **Corrigé** : à consulter seulement après
6. **Vocabulaire** : les thèmes du module, la liste complète est dans le tableur

Comment l'utiliser sur 4 semaines :

- **Semaines 1 à 3** : une leçon par semaine, 20 nouveaux mots par semaine, exercices en fin de semaine
- **Semaine 4** : refaire tous les exercices, réviser les cartes ratées, mini-test

Pour l'appli : chaque leçon devient une mini-leçon, chaque exercice une « phrase à traduire » ou un QCM, et chaque liste de vocabulaire un paquet de cartes.

# Niveau A1

## Module 1 — Moi et les autres (semaines 1 à 4)

**Objectifs** : te présenter, épeler ton nom, donner ton âge, ta nationalité et ton métier, poser les mêmes questions à quelqu'un.

### Leçon 1 : les pronoms et le verbe *to be* (être)

*To be* sert à dire qui on est, comment on va, son âge, sa nationalité et son métier.

| Pronom | Affirmatif | Forme courte | Négatif | Question |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| I (je) | I am | I'm | I'm not | Am I…? |
| you (tu / vous) | you are | you're | you aren't | Are you…? |
| he (il) | he is | he's | he isn't | Is he…? |
| she (elle) | she is | she's | she isn't | Is she…? |
| it (il / elle, chose ou animal) | it is | it's | it isn't | Is it…? |
| we (nous) | we are | we're | we aren't | Are we…? |
| they (ils / elles) | they are | they're | they aren't | Are they…? |

Exemples :

- *I'm Erika. I'm 21.* → Je suis Erika. J'ai 21 ans.
- *She's French.* → Elle est française.
- *Are you a student? — Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.* → Tu es étudiant ? — Oui. / Non.

À retenir : l'âge se dit avec *to be* : *I am 21* (et jamais *I have 21*).

### Leçon 2 : les articles et le pluriel

| Article | Quand l'utiliser | Exemple |
| --- | --- | --- |
| a | Un / une, devant un son consonne | a student, a car, a university (son « you ») |
| an | Un / une, devant un son voyelle | an engineer, an apple, an hour (le h ne se prononce pas) |
| the | Le / la / les, quelque chose de précis | the teacher, the robots |

Le métier prend toujours *a / an* : *I'm an engineer* (je suis ingénieure).

Pluriel : on ajoute **-s** (*a car → two cars*). Cas particuliers :

- -s, -sh, -ch, -x → **-es** : bus → buses, watch → watches, box → boxes
- consonne + y → **-ies** : city → cities, baby → babies
- irréguliers : man → men, woman → women, child → children, person → people, foot → feet, tooth → teeth

### Leçon 3 : se présenter et poser des questions

| Question | Réponse |
| --- | --- |
| What's your name? | My name is… / I'm… |
| How do you spell it? | E-R-I-K-A |
| How old are you? | I'm 21 (years old). |
| Where are you from? | I'm from France. I'm French. |
| What do you do? / What's your job? | I'm a student. I'm an engineer. |
| How are you? | I'm fine, thanks. And you? |
| Nice to meet you. | Nice to meet you too. |

L'alphabet, les lettres qui piègent les francophones : **A** (« ei »), **E** (« i »), **I** (« aï »), **G** (« dji »), **J** (« djei »), **R** (« ar »), **W** (« double you »), **Y** (« waï »).

### Pièges à éviter

- ✗ *I have 21 years* → ✓ *I am 21*
- ✗ *I am engineer* → ✓ *I am an engineer*
- ✗ *She is french* → ✓ *She is French* (majuscule aux nationalités et aux langues)
- ✗ *The peoples* → ✓ *the people* (déjà pluriel)

### Exercices

1. Complète avec *am, is* ou *are* : a) I \_\_\_ tired. b) They \_\_\_ from Spain. c) My brother \_\_\_ a teacher. d) We \_\_\_ students.
2. Mets à la forme négative : a) He is English. b) You are late. c) I am a doctor.
3. *A* ou *an* ? a) \_\_\_ orange b) \_\_\_ robot c) \_\_\_ hour d) \_\_\_ university e) \_\_\_ engineer
4. Mets au pluriel : a) a box b) a city c) a child d) a woman e) a key
5. Traduis en anglais : a) Je m'appelle Léa et j'ai 25 ans. b) Il est italien. c) Vous êtes ingénieurs ? d) Ce n'est pas mon livre.
6. Écris 5 phrases pour te présenter (nom, âge, ville, nationalité, métier).

### Corrigé

1. a) am b) are c) is d) are
2. a) He isn't English. b) You aren't late. c) I'm not a doctor.
3. a) an b) a c) an d) a e) an
4. a) boxes b) cities c) children d) women e) keys (voyelle + y → simplement -s)
5. a) My name is Léa and I'm 25. b) He's Italian. c) Are you engineers? d) It isn't my book.
6. Exemple : *My name is Erika. I'm 21. I live in Bordeaux. I'm French. I'm a robotics student.*

### Vocabulaire du module

Salutations, nombres de 0 à 100, pays et nationalités, famille, métiers. Liste complète dans le tableur, niveau A1, module 1.

## Module 2 — Mon quotidien (semaines 5 à 8)

**Objectifs** : décrire ta journée type, dire l'heure et la date, parler de tes habitudes et poser des questions simples.

### Leçon 1 : le présent simple

Le présent simple sert pour les **habitudes**, les **vérités générales** et les **emplois du temps**.

|  | Affirmatif | Négatif | Question |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| I / you / we / they | I work | I don't work | Do you work? |
| he / she / it | she work**s** | she doesn't work | Does she work? |

Règle d'or : avec **he / she / it**, on ajoute **-s** au verbe à l'affirmatif. Au négatif et en question, le -s passe sur *does* et le verbe reste nu (*She doesn't work*, jamais *She doesn't works*).

Orthographe du -s :

- -s, -sh, -ch, -x, -o → **-es** : watches, goes, does, fixes
- consonne + y → **-ies** : study → studies (mais play → plays)
- *have* → **has**

Exemples :

- *I get up at 7 a.m.* → Je me lève à 7 h.
- *He doesn't drink coffee.* → Il ne boit pas de café.
- *Do you live in Bordeaux? — Yes, I do.*

### Leçon 2 : les adverbes de fréquence

| Adverbe | Sens | Fréquence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| always | toujours | 100 % |
| usually | d'habitude | 80 % |
| often | souvent | 60 % |
| sometimes | parfois | 40 % |
| rarely | rarement | 10 % |
| never | jamais | 0 % |

Place : **avant** le verbe principal (*I often cook*) mais **après** *to be* (*I'm always late*).

Pour demander : *How often do you…?* → *Once a week, twice a month, every day.*

### Leçon 3 : l'heure, les jours et les dates

| Question | Réponse |
| --- | --- |
| What time is it? | It's eight o'clock / It's half past eight (8 h 30) / It's quarter to nine (8 h 45) / It's ten past nine (9 h 10) |
| What day is it today? | It's Monday. |
| When is your birthday? | It's on May 3rd. |

Prépositions de temps :

- **at** + heure : *at 7 o'clock, at night, at the weekend* (anglais britannique)
- **on** + jour / date : *on Monday, on July 14th*
- **in** + mois, année, saison, partie de la journée : *in June, in 2026, in winter, in the morning*

*a.m.* = le matin (minuit à midi), *p.m.* = l'après-midi et le soir. Jours et mois prennent une **majuscule** : *Monday, January*.

### Leçon 4 : *have got* et *have*

*I have a car* = *I've got a car* (j'ai une voiture). *Have got* est surtout britannique et oral.

|  | have | have got |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Négatif | I don't have a car | I haven't got a car |
| Question | Do you have a car? | Have you got a car? |

### Pièges à éviter

- ✗ *She work in a bank* → ✓ *She works in a bank*
- ✗ *Does he works?* → ✓ *Does he work?*
- ✗ *I go always to the gym* → ✓ *I always go to the gym*
- ✗ *in Monday* → ✓ *on Monday*
- ✗ *I am agree* → ✓ *I agree* (*agree* est un verbe)

### Exercices

1. Conjugue au présent simple : a) She (watch) TV every evening. b) My father (fix) cars. c) They (not / like) coffee. d) (you / speak) English?
2. Place l'adverbe : a) I am tired in the morning. (always) b) We go to the cinema. (sometimes) c) He eats meat. (never)
3. *At, on* ou *in* ? a) \_\_\_ Friday b) \_\_\_ 6 p.m. c) \_\_\_ August d) \_\_\_ the evening e) \_\_\_ Christmas Day
4. Écris l'heure en lettres : a) 7 h 00 b) 9 h 30 c) 10 h 15 d) 4 h 45
5. Traduis : a) Elle ne travaille pas le dimanche. b) Tu te lèves à quelle heure ? c) Nous allons souvent au restaurant. d) Il a deux frères.
6. Décris ta journée type en 8 phrases (réveil, petit-déjeuner, transport, travail, sport, dîner, coucher).

### Corrigé

1. a) watches b) fixes c) don't like d) Do you speak
2. a) I am always tired in the morning. b) We sometimes go to the cinema. c) He never eats meat.
3. a) on b) at c) in d) in e) on
4. a) seven o'clock b) half past nine c) quarter past ten d) quarter to five
5. a) She doesn't work on Sundays. b) What time do you get up? c) We often go to the restaurant. (ou *We often eat out.*) d) He has two brothers. / He's got two brothers.
6. Exemple : *I get up at 7. I have breakfast at 7:30. I take the tram to work…*

### Vocabulaire du module

Jours, mois, heures, routine quotidienne, repas, couleurs, vêtements. Liste complète dans le tableur, niveau A1, module 2.

## Module 3 — Autour de moi (semaines 9 à 12)

**Objectifs** : décrire ton logement et ta ville, situer des objets, donner et comprendre des consignes, dire ce que tu sais faire.

### Leçon 1 : *there is / there are* (il y a)

|  | Singulier | Pluriel |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Affirmatif | There is (there's) a park. | There are two shops. |
| Négatif | There isn't a station. | There aren't any buses. |
| Question | Is there a bank? | Are there any restaurants? |

*Some* (des) à l'affirmatif, *any* au négatif et en question : *There are some trees. / There aren't any trees. / Are there any trees?*

### Leçon 2 : les prépositions de lieu

| Préposition | Sens | Exemple |
| --- | --- | --- |
| in | dans | The keys are in the bag. |
| on | sur (en contact) | The phone is on the table. |
| under | sous | The cat is under the bed. |
| next to | à côté de | The bank is next to the café. |
| between | entre | The shop is between the bank and the school. |
| in front of | devant | The car is in front of the house. |
| behind | derrière | The garden is behind the house. |
| opposite | en face de | The station is opposite the hotel. |
| above / below | au-dessus de / en dessous de | The lamp is above the desk. |

### Leçon 3 : *can / can't* (savoir, pouvoir)

*Can* ne change jamais, il est suivi du verbe sans *to* : *I can swim. She can drive. They can't come.*

|  | Exemple |
| --- | --- |
| Capacité | I can speak three languages. |
| Permission | Can I open the window? |
| Demande | Can you help me, please? |

✗ *She cans* ✗ *I can to swim* → ✓ *She can*, ✓ *I can swim*.

### Leçon 4 : l'impératif (les consignes)

Verbe seul pour donner un ordre ou une consigne, *don't* + verbe pour une interdiction. *Let's* + verbe = proposition (allons-y).

- *Sit down. Stand up. Open your books. Listen.*
- *Turn left. Go straight on. Take the second street on the right.*
- *Don't touch it. Don't run.*
- *Let's go! Let's start.*

C'est exactement la forme des consignes de sport : *Stretch your arms. Bend your knees. Don't stop!*

### Leçon 5 : les possessifs

| Pronom | Adjectif possessif | Exemple |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I | my | my house |
| you | your | your phone |
| he | his | his car (sa voiture, à lui) |
| she | her | her car (sa voiture, à elle) |
| it | its | its tail |
| we | our | our team |
| they | their | their parents |

Le **'s** pour l'appartenance : *Tom's bike* (le vélo de Tom), *my parents' house* (pluriel en -s : apostrophe seule).

Attention : en anglais le possessif s'accorde avec le **possesseur** : *his mother* (la mère de lui), *her father* (le père d'elle).

### Pièges à éviter

- ✗ *There is two cars* → ✓ *There are two cars*
- ✗ *It has a park near my house* → ✓ *There is a park near my house*
- ✗ *Its my car* → ✓ *It's my car* (*it's* = it is ; *its* = son, sa pour une chose)
- ✗ *Marie and his brother* → ✓ *Marie and her brother*

### Exercices

1. *There is* ou *there are* ? a) \_\_\_ a supermarket near here. b) \_\_\_ three bedrooms. c) \_\_\_ any milk? d) \_\_\_ some students in the room.
2. Complète avec une préposition : a) The book is \_\_\_ the desk. b) The dog sleeps \_\_\_ the table. c) The pharmacy is \_\_\_ the bank and the post office. d) The bus stop is \_\_\_ the school (en face).
3. Choisis le possessif : a) Paul loves \_\_\_ sister. b) Julie lives with \_\_\_ parents. c) We like \_\_\_ new flat. d) The cat eats \_\_\_ food.
4. Mets à l'impératif : a) (ne pas parler) during the test. b) (tourner) right at the traffic lights. c) (on fait une pause) a break.
5. Traduis : a) Il y a une boulangerie en face de chez moi. b) Est-ce que tu sais nager ? c) Les clés sont sous le livre. d) C'est la voiture de mon frère.
6. Décris ton appartement en 6 phrases avec *there is / there are* et des prépositions.

### Corrigé

1. a) There is b) There are c) Is there d) There are
2. a) on b) under c) between d) opposite
3. a) his b) her c) our d) its
4. a) Don't talk b) Turn c) Let's take
5. a) There is a bakery opposite my house. b) Can you swim? c) The keys are under the book. d) It's my brother's car.
6. Exemple : *There are three rooms in my flat. There is a sofa in the living room. My desk is next to the window…*

### Vocabulaire du module

Maison et pièces, mobilier, ville et magasins, transports, parties du corps, verbes de mouvement. Liste complète dans le tableur, niveau A1, module 3.

# Niveau A2

## Module 4 — Ce qui se passe maintenant (semaines 13 à 16)

**Objectifs** : dire ce que tu fais en ce moment, distinguer habitude et action en cours, parler de tes goûts, de la météo et de tes émotions.

### Leçon 1 : le présent continu (be + -ing)

Il sert pour une action **en train de se faire** maintenant, une situation **temporaire**, ou un **projet déjà organisé**.

|  | Exemple |
| --- | --- |
| Affirmatif | I'm working. She's running. They're eating. |
| Négatif | I'm not working. He isn't listening. |
| Question | Are you coming? What is she doing? |

Orthographe du -ing :

- -e muet → on l'enlève : make → making, write → writing
- 1 syllabe consonne-voyelle-consonne → on double : run → running, stop → stopping, swim → swimming
- -ie → -ying : lie → lying

Mots-repères : *now, right now, at the moment, today, this week, look!, listen!*

Projet organisé : *I'm meeting a client tomorrow.* (c'est dans l'agenda)

### Leçon 2 : présent simple ou présent continu ?

| Présent simple | Présent continu |
| --- | --- |
| Habitude : *I go to the gym on Mondays.* | Maintenant : *I'm going to the gym now.* |
| Permanent : *She lives in Lyon.* | Temporaire : *She's living with her parents this month.* |
| Vérité générale : *Water boils at 100 °C.* | Changement en cours : *Prices are going up.* |

Verbes d'état, presque jamais au continu : *like, love, hate, want, need, know, understand, believe, remember, belong, seem*. ✗ *I'm knowing* → ✓ *I know*.

### Leçon 3 : parler de ses goûts

*like, love, enjoy, hate, don't mind* + **verbe en -ing** :

- *I love cooking. He hates waiting. I don't mind driving.*
- *Would like* (aimerait) + **to** : *I'd like to travel to Japan.* (souhait, pas une habitude)
- *I'm into…* (familier) : *I'm into rock climbing.*
- *I'm interested in robotics. I'm good at maths. I'm bad at drawing.* (adjectif + préposition + nom ou -ing)

### Leçon 4 : la météo et les émotions

- *What's the weather like? — It's sunny / rainy / cloudy / windy / foggy. It's raining. It's snowing.*
- *It's hot / warm / cool / cold / freezing.*
- *How do you feel? — I feel tired / happy / stressed / nervous / excited / bored.*

Adjectifs en **-ed** = ce que tu ressens, en **-ing** = ce qui provoque : *I'm bored* (je m'ennuie) / *The film is boring* (le film est ennuyeux).

### Pièges à éviter

- ✗ *I am working every day* → ✓ *I work every day*
- ✗ *What do you do now?* (= quel est ton métier) → ✓ *What are you doing now?*
- ✗ *I am boring* (je suis ennuyeux) → ✓ *I am bored* (je m'ennuie)
- ✗ *I like to play football* n'est pas faux, mais *I like playing football* est plus naturel pour une habitude en anglais britannique

### Exercices

1. Mets au présent continu : a) Look! It (snow). b) We (not / watch) TV. c) What (you / do)? d) She (write) an email.
2. Simple ou continu ? a) I (drink) coffee every morning. b) Be quiet, the baby (sleep). c) He usually (take) the bus but today he (walk). d) I (not / understand) this word.
3. Complète avec *-ed* ou *-ing* : a) The match was very excit\_\_\_. b) I'm tir\_\_\_ after work. c) This lesson is interest\_\_\_. d) She's worri\_\_\_ about the exam.
4. Traduis : a) Il pleut, prends un parapluie. b) J'adore courir le matin. c) Qu'est-ce que tu fais ce soir ? d) Je m'ennuie.
5. Regarde par la fenêtre et écris 5 phrases sur ce qui se passe.

### Corrigé

1. a) It's snowing b) We aren't watching c) are you doing d) She's writing
2. a) drink b) is sleeping c) takes / is walking d) don't understand
3. a) exciting b) tired c) interesting d) worried
4. a) It's raining, take an umbrella. b) I love running in the morning. c) What are you doing tonight? d) I'm bored.
5. Exemple : *A man is walking his dog. Two children are playing. It's raining…*

### Vocabulaire du module

Loisirs, sports, météo, émotions, verbes d'action. Liste complète dans le tableur, niveau A2, module 4.

## Module 5 — Raconter le passé (semaines 17 à 20)

**Objectifs** : raconter ton week-end, un voyage ou un souvenir, situer un événement dans le temps et poser des questions sur le passé.

### Leçon 1 : le prétérit de *to be* (was / were)

|  | Exemple |
| --- | --- |
| I / he / she / it | I was tired. She wasn't at home. Was it good? |
| you / we / they | We were in London. They weren't late. Were you happy? |

*Where were you yesterday? — I was at work.* / *There was a problem. There were many people.*

### Leçon 2 : le prétérit des verbes réguliers

On ajoute **-ed**, et c'est la **même forme pour toutes les personnes** : *I worked, she worked, they worked.*

|  | Exemple |
| --- | --- |
| Affirmatif | I visited Rome. |
| Négatif | I **didn't** visit Rome. (verbe nu) |
| Question | **Did** you visit Rome? (verbe nu) |

Orthographe : *live → lived, stop → stopped, study → studied, play → played.*

Prononciation du -ed :

- « t » après p, k, f, s, sh, ch : *worked, stopped, watched*
- « d » après les autres sons : *played, lived, called*
- « id » après t et d : *wanted, needed, started*

### Leçon 4 : situer dans le passé

- *yesterday, last night, last week, last year, in 2019, two days ago, when I was a child*
- **ago** se place **après** la durée : *three years ago* (il y a trois ans)
- Connecteurs pour raconter : *first, then, after that, finally*

### Pièges à éviter

- ✗ *I didn't went* → ✓ *I didn't go*
- ✗ *Did you saw him?* → ✓ *Did you see him?*
- ✗ *Ago three years* → ✓ *three years ago*
- ✗ *The last week* → ✓ *last week* (sans *the*)

### Exercices

5. Raconte ton dernier week-end en 8 phrases avec *first, then, after that, finally*.

## Module 6 — En voyage (semaines 21 à 24)

**Objectifs** : réserver et gérer une chambre d'hôtel, commander au restaurant, demander ton chemin, te débrouiller à l'aéroport et parler de tes projets de voyage.

### Leçon 1 : le futur avec *going to* et *will*

| Forme | Quand | Exemple |
| --- | --- | --- |
| be going to + verbe | Projet déjà décidé | *I'm going to visit London in July.* |
| be going to + verbe | Prédiction avec un indice visible | *Look at the clouds, it's going to rain.* |
| will + verbe | Décision prise sur le moment | *I'll take the blue one.* |
| will + verbe | Promesse, offre | *I'll help you. I'll call you back.* |
| will + verbe | Prédiction, opinion | *I think it will be expensive.* |

Négatif : *won't* (= will not) : *I won't be late.*

### Leçon 2 : demander poliment

En anglais, la politesse passe par le **modal** et par *please*. *Can I…?* est correct, *Could I…?* est plus poli.

| Je veux… | Formule polie |
| --- | --- |
| demander quelque chose | *Could I have the menu, please?* |
| commander | *I'd like the chicken, please.* / *I'll have a coffee.* |
| demander un service | *Could you call a taxi for me, please?* |
| demander la permission | *Is it OK if I pay by card?* |
| demander une information | *Could you tell me where the station is?* |

*I'd like* = *I would like* (je voudrais) : toujours suivi d'un nom ou de **to** + verbe.

### Leçon 3 : les quantités

|  | Indénombrable (milk, water, money, bread) | Dénombrable (apples, rooms, euros) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Beaucoup (négatif, question) | much : *Is there much traffic?* | many : *How many rooms?* |
| Un peu / quelques | a little : *a little sugar* | a few : *a few days* |
| Combien ? | How much is it? How much water? | How many nights? |
| Beaucoup (affirmatif) | a lot of / lots of | a lot of / lots of |

Attention : *information, advice, luggage, furniture* sont **indénombrables** en anglais : *some information* (jamais *informations*).

### Leçon 4 : dialogues types

**À l'hôtel**

- *Hi, I have a reservation under the name Lajus.*
- *I'd like to book a double room for two nights.*
- *What time is check-out? Is breakfast included?*
- *The air conditioning doesn't work. Could someone have a look at it?*
- *Could I have a late check-out, please?*

**Au restaurant**

- *A table for two, please. — Do you have a reservation?*
- *Could we see the menu, please? What do you recommend?*
- *I'm allergic to nuts. Is there any gluten in this dish?*
- *Could we have the bill, please? Can I pay by card?* (US : *the check*)

**Demander son chemin**

- *Excuse me, how do I get to the station?*
- *Go straight on, turn left at the traffic lights, it's on your right.*
- *Is it far from here? — No, it's a five-minute walk.*

**À l'aéroport**

- *Where is the check-in desk for Air France?*
- *Can I take this bag on board? — Yes, it's hand luggage.*
- *My flight is delayed. Which gate is it?*
- *I'd like a window seat / an aisle seat, please.*

### Pièges à éviter

- ✗ *I want a coffee* (sec, presque impoli) → ✓ *Could I have a coffee, please?*
- ✗ *How much people?* → ✓ *How many people?*
- ✗ *an information* → ✓ *a piece of information* / *some information*
- ✗ *I will to go* → ✓ *I will go*

### Exercices

1. *Going to* ou *will* ? a) We've booked the tickets: we \_\_\_ fly to Rome on Friday. b) The phone is ringing. — I \_\_\_ answer it! c) Look at that car, it \_\_\_ crash! d) I promise I \_\_\_ be on time.

## Module 7 — Comparer et décrire (semaines 25 à 28)

**Objectifs** : comparer deux produits ou deux villes, décrire une personne, donner un conseil ou une obligation, préciser la manière de faire.

### Leçon 1 : comparatifs et superlatifs

| Adjectif | Comparatif | Superlatif |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Court (1 syllabe) : fast | fast**er** than | the fast**est** |
| Terminé par -y : easy | eas**ier** than | the eas**iest** |
| Long (2+ syllabes) : expensive | **more** expensive than | **the most** expensive |
| Irrégulier : good | better than | the best |
| Irrégulier : bad | worse than | the worst |
| Irrégulier : far | further than | the furthest |

Orthographe : *big → bigger, hot → hotter* (on double la consonne finale après une voyelle courte).

Autres structures :

- Égalité : *as fast as* (aussi rapide que), *not as expensive as* (moins cher que)
- Infériorité : *less expensive than* (moins cher que)
- *The more you practise, the better you speak.* (plus… plus…, revu au module 19)

Exemples : *This laptop is lighter than mine. It's the best restaurant in town. Bordeaux is not as big as Paris.*

### Leçon 2 : conseil et obligation

| Modal | Sens | Exemple |
| --- | --- | --- |
| should / shouldn't | conseil (tu devrais) | *You should drink more water.* |
| must | obligation ressentie, règle forte | *I must finish this today.* |
| mustn't | interdiction | *You mustn't smoke here.* |
| have to | obligation extérieure | *I have to wear a uniform at work.* |
| don't have to | absence d'obligation | *You don't have to come.* (ce n'est pas obligatoire) |

Piège majeur : *mustn't* = interdit ; *don't have to* = pas obligé. Ce n'est **pas** la même chose.

Au passé, *must* devient **had to** : *I had to work late yesterday.*

### Leçon 3 : les adverbes de manière

Adjectif + **-ly** : *slow → slowly, careful → carefully, quick → quickly, easy → easily.*

Irréguliers : *good → well, fast → fast, hard → hard, late → late.* ✗ *He drives good* → ✓ *He drives well*.

L'adverbe se place **après le verbe ou le complément** : *Speak slowly. Lift the box carefully. She finished the project quickly.* Idéal pour les consignes de coach : *Breathe slowly. Move carefully.*

### Leçon 4 : décrire une personne

- Physique : *She's tall / short / slim. He has short brown hair and blue eyes. He has a beard.*
- Âge : *in her twenties* (la vingtaine), *middle-aged, elderly*
- Caractère : *friendly, shy, funny, patient, hard-working, reliable, lazy, rude*
- *What does he look like?* = à quoi il ressemble (physique) / *What is he like?* = comment il est (caractère)

### Pièges à éviter

- ✗ *more big* → ✓ *bigger*
- ✗ *more better* → ✓ *better* (jamais *more* avec un comparatif en -er)
- ✗ *You must not to come* → ✓ *You don't have to come* (si ce n'est pas obligatoire)
- ✗ *She has long hairs* → ✓ *She has long hair* (indénombrable)

### Exercices

1. Comparatif ou superlatif : a) A plane is (fast) than a train. b) It's (expensive) hotel in the city. c) Today is (bad) day of my life. d) My flat is (small) than yours.
2. *Should, must, mustn't* ou *don't have to* ? a) You \_\_\_ touch that, it's dangerous! b) You look tired, you \_\_\_ go to bed. c) It's free, you \_\_\_ pay. d) I \_\_\_ remember to call the client.
3. Transforme en adverbe : a) She sings (beautiful). b) Drive (careful). c) He speaks English (good). d) The time passes (quick).
4. Traduis : a) Ce téléphone est moins cher que l'autre. b) Tu devrais faire du sport. c) Nous ne sommes pas obligés de partir tôt. d) Il est aussi grand que son père.
5. Compare deux villes que tu connais en 6 phrases.

### Corrigé

1. a) faster b) the most expensive c) the worst d) smaller
2. a) mustn't b) should c) don't have to d) must
3. a) beautifully b) carefully c) well d) quickly
4. a) This phone is cheaper than the other one. b) You should do some sport. (ou *You should exercise.*) c) We don't have to leave early. d) He's as tall as his father.
5. Exemple : *Paris is bigger than Bordeaux, but Bordeaux is less expensive. The weather is better in Bordeaux…*

Santé et corps, conseils, achats, description physique et caractère, adverbes. Liste complète dans le tableur, niveau A2, module 7.

## Module 8 — Expériences (semaines 29 à 32)

**Objectifs** : parler de ce que tu as déjà fait dans ta vie, d'un résultat récent, d'une durée qui continue, et savoir quand utiliser le prétérit à la place.

### Leçon 1 : le present perfect (have + participe passé)

|  | Exemple |
| --- | --- |
| Affirmatif | I've (I have) visited Japan. She's (she has) finished. |
| Négatif | I haven't seen this film. He hasn't called. |
| Question | Have you ever been to London? Has she arrived? |

Le participe passé : *-ed* pour les verbes réguliers, troisième colonne du tableau du module 5 pour les irréguliers.

Il relie le **passé au présent**. Trois usages :

1. **Expérience de vie** (sans date) : *I've climbed a mountain. Have you ever eaten sushi?*
2. **Résultat visible maintenant** : *I've lost my keys* (je ne peux pas rentrer). *She's broken her arm.*
3. **Durée qui continue** : *I've lived here for 5 years* (et j'y vis toujours).

### Leçon 2 : les petits mots du present perfect

| Mot | Sens | Place | Exemple |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ever | déjà (dans ta vie), en question | avant le participe | *Have you ever driven a truck?* |
| never | jamais | avant le participe | *I've never been to Asia.* |
| already | déjà (plus tôt que prévu) | avant le participe | *I've already sent the email.* |
| yet | pas encore (négatif) / déjà (question) | fin de phrase | *I haven't finished yet. Have you finished yet?* |
| just | venir de | avant le participe | *He's just left.* (il vient de partir) |

### Leçon 3 : *for* et *since*

- **for** + durée : *for two hours, for three years, for a long time*
- **since** + point de départ : *since Monday, since 2020, since I was a child*

*How long have you worked at KUKA? — I've worked there since September / for two months.*

Le français dit « je travaille ici **depuis** 2 ans » au présent, l'anglais met le present perfect : ✗ *I work here since 2 years* → ✓ *I've worked here for 2 years*.

### Leçon 4 : present perfect ou prétérit ?

| Prétérit | Present perfect |
| --- | --- |
| Moment **précis et terminé** | Moment **non précisé** ou lien avec maintenant |
| *I went to Rome in 2022.* | *I've been to Rome.* |
| *I lost my keys yesterday.* | *I've lost my keys.* (toujours perdues) |
| *She worked there for 3 years.* (elle n'y est plus) | *She's worked there for 3 years.* (elle y est encore) |

Règle simple : si la phrase contient *yesterday, last…, ago, in 2020, when…*, c'est le **prétérit**.

*Been* ou *gone* ? *He's been to Paris* (il y est allé et revenu) / *He's gone to Paris* (il y est, il n'est pas là).

### Pièges à éviter

- ✗ *I have seen him yesterday* → ✓ *I saw him yesterday*
- ✗ *I live here since 2020* → ✓ *I've lived here since 2020*
- ✗ *since two years* → ✓ *for two years*
- ✗ *Did you ever go to Spain?* → ✓ *Have you ever been to Spain?*

### Exercices

1. Mets au present perfect : a) I (finish) my report. b) She (never / see) snow. c) (you / ever / meet) a famous person? d) They (not / arrive) yet.
2. *For* ou *since* ? a) \_\_\_ 2019 b) \_\_\_ ten minutes c) \_\_\_ last summer d) \_\_\_ ages e) \_\_\_ I started this job
3. Prétérit ou present perfect ? a) I (buy) a new car last week. b) I (work) here for six months and I love it. c) (you / see) the new Batman film? — Yes, I (see) it on Saturday. d) Oh no! I (forget) my password.
4. Place *already, yet* ou *just* : a) I haven't called him. b) She's arrived, she's taking off her coat. c) Have you done your homework? d) We've paid, it's done.
5. Traduis : a) J'habite à Bordeaux depuis trois ans. b) Tu as déjà travaillé avec des robots ? c) Je viens de recevoir ton mail. d) Il n'a pas encore répondu.
6. Écris 6 phrases sur tes expériences avec *I've… / I've never…*.

### Corrigé

1. a) I've finished b) She's never seen c) Have you ever met d) haven't arrived
2. a) since b) for c) since d) for e) since
3. a) bought b) have worked c) Have you seen / saw d) have forgotten
4. a) I haven't called him yet. b) She's just arrived… c) Have you done your homework yet? (ou *already*, si surpris) d) We've already paid.
5. a) I've lived in Bordeaux for three years. b) Have you ever worked with robots? c) I've just received your email. d) He hasn't answered yet.

## Module 9 — Raconter une histoire (semaines 33 à 36)

**Objectifs** : raconter une anecdote vivante avec un décor et des événements, parler de tes anciennes habitudes, enchaîner les étapes d'un récit.

### Leçon 1 : le prétérit continu (was / were + -ing)

Il décrit une action **en cours à un moment du passé**, souvent interrompue par une action courte au prétérit simple.

|  | Exemple |
| --- | --- |
| Affirmatif | I was driving. They were sleeping. |
| Négatif | She wasn't listening. |
| Question | What were you doing at 8 p.m.? |

- **Le décor** (continu) + **l'événement** (simple) : *I was cooking when the phone rang.*
- **Deux actions en parallèle** : *While I was working, he was watching TV.*
- **When** + action courte, **while** + action longue : *When the alarm went off, we were having a meeting. / The alarm went off while we were having a meeting.*

### Leçon 2 : *used to* (avant, j'avais l'habitude de)

*Used to* + verbe = une habitude ou un état du passé **qui n'existe plus**.

|  | Exemple |
| --- | --- |
| Affirmatif | I used to play the piano. (avant, je jouais du piano) |
| Négatif | I didn't use to like vegetables. |
| Question | Did you use to live in Paris? |

Attention : au négatif et en question, *use to* sans *-d*.

Ne pas confondre avec *be used to + -ing* (être habitué à) : *I'm used to working at night.* (B2)

### Leçon 3 : les connecteurs du récit

| Étape | Connecteurs |
| --- | --- |
| Début | *One day… / Last summer… / A few years ago… / At first…* |
| Suite | *Then… / After that… / Next… / Later… / Meanwhile* (pendant ce temps) |
| Surprise | *Suddenly… / All of a sudden… / Unfortunately… / Luckily…* |
| Fin | *In the end… / Finally… / Eventually…* (finalement, après un long moment) |

Attention aux faux amis : *eventually* = finalement (pas « éventuellement », qui se dit *possibly*), *actually* = en fait (pas « actuellement », qui se dit *currently*).

### Leçon 4 : structurer une anecdote

1. **Situer** : quand, où, avec qui (prétérit continu pour le décor)
2. **L'événement** : ce qui s'est passé (prétérit simple + *suddenly*)
3. **La réaction** : ce que tu as fait, ressenti
4. **La fin** : comment ça s'est terminé (*in the end*)

Exemple : *Last year, I was travelling in Scotland with friends. It was raining and we were looking for our hotel. Suddenly, our car broke down in the middle of nowhere. Luckily, a farmer stopped and helped us. In the end, we arrived at midnight, but it's one of my best memories.*

### Pièges à éviter

- ✗ *When I was arriving, he left* → ✓ *When I arrived, he was leaving* (ou *he left*, selon le sens)
- ✗ *I didn't used to* → ✓ *I didn't use to*
- ✗ *Actually, I live in Bordeaux* (pour « actuellement ») → ✓ *Currently, I live in Bordeaux*
- ✗ *I was knowing* → ✓ *I knew* (verbe d'état)

### Exercices

1. Prétérit simple ou continu ? a) I (walk) home when I (see) an accident. b) What (you / do) when I (call) you? c) While she (study), her brother (play) video games. d) The lights (go) out while we (have) dinner.
2. Écris avec *used to* : a) Avant, il fumait. b) Quand j'étais petite, je n'aimais pas le poisson. c) Est-ce que tu habitais à la campagne avant ?
3. Choisis le bon connecteur : a) We waited for two hours and \_\_\_ the bus arrived. (eventually / suddenly) b) I was reading \_\_\_ someone knocked on the door. (when / while) c) \_\_\_, nobody was hurt. (Luckily / Actually)
4. Traduis : a) Je dormais quand le réveil a sonné. b) En fait, je ne suis pas d'accord. c) Pendant que je cuisinais, il a mis la table. d) Avant, on allait à la plage tous les étés.
5. Raconte une anecdote (voyage, panne, rencontre) en 10 phrases avec la structure de la leçon 4.

### Corrigé

1. a) was walking / saw b) were you doing / called c) was studying / was playing d) went / were having
2. a) He used to smoke. b) When I was a child, I didn't use to like fish. c) Did you use to live in the countryside?
3. a) eventually b) when c) Luckily
4. a) I was sleeping when the alarm went off. b) Actually, I don't agree. c) While I was cooking, he set the table. d) We used to go to the beach every summer.
5. Réponse libre. Vérifie : décor au continu, événements au simple, au moins 4 connecteurs.

## Module 10 — Hypothèses (semaines 37 à 40)

**Objectifs** : parler de tes projets et de ce qui pourrait arriver, exprimer des conditions réelles, nuancer une probabilité.

### Leçon 1 : le conditionnel 0 (vérités générales)

**If + présent, présent** : quand la condition est remplie, le résultat arrive toujours.

- *If you heat water to 100 °C, it boils.*
- *If the battery is low, the robot stops.*
- *If I drink coffee at night, I can't sleep.*

*If* peut être remplacé par *when* sans changer le sens.

### Leçon 2 : le conditionnel 1 (situations possibles)

**If + présent, will + verbe** : une condition réaliste dans le futur.

- *If it rains tomorrow, we'll stay at home.*
- *If you send me the file, I'll check it today.*
- *If we don't hurry, we'll miss the train.*

Règle d'or : **jamais de *will* après *if*** (ni après *when, as soon as, before, after, until*).

✗ *If it will rain* → ✓ *If it rains* ✗ *When I will arrive, I'll call you* → ✓ *When I arrive, I'll call you*

*Unless* = *if… not* (à moins que) : *We'll be late unless we leave now.*

### Leçon 3 : la probabilité avec *might / may / could / will probably*

| Probabilité | Forme | Exemple |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Presque sûr | will definitely | *It will definitely be ready on Monday.* |
| Probable | will probably / is likely to | *He'll probably come. She's likely to win.* |
| Possible | may / might / could | *It might rain. The delivery may be late.* |
| Peu probable | probably won't / is unlikely to | *They probably won't agree.* |
| Impossible | definitely won't | *It definitely won't work.* |

Place de *probably* : **après** *will*, mais **avant** *won't* : *He'll probably come / He probably won't come.*

### Leçon 4 : le futur continu et les projets

**Will be + -ing** : une action en cours à un moment du futur.

- *This time next week, I'll be lying on the beach.*
- *Don't call at 10, I'll be having a meeting.*

Pour parler de projets : *I'm planning to… / I'm thinking of + -ing / I hope to… / My goal is to…*

*I'm planning to become a project manager. I'm thinking of learning German. I hope to work abroad one day.*

### Pièges à éviter

- ✗ *If I will have time, I will come* → ✓ *If I have time, I'll come*
- ✗ *He will probably not come* (lourd) → ✓ *He probably won't come*
- ✗ *Maybe it will might rain* → ✓ *It might rain* ou *Maybe it will rain*
- ✗ *I'm thinking to learn* → ✓ *I'm thinking of learning*

### Exercices

1. Conditionnel 0 ou 1 : a) If you (press) this button, the machine (start). (toujours) b) If he (call) me, I (tell) him the news. c) If we (not / test) the prototype, we (not / find) the bug.
2. Corrige : a) When I will finish, I'll send it. b) If it will be sunny, we go to the beach. c) Unless you don't study, you'll fail.
3. Exprime la probabilité : a) Il va peut-être neiger. b) Elle va probablement réussir. c) Ils ne vont sûrement pas venir.
4. Traduis : a) Si tu as besoin d'aide, appelle-moi. b) Je serai en réunion à 15 h. c) Si le projet est validé, nous commencerons en janvier. d) Je pense apprendre l'allemand.
5. Écris 6 phrases sur tes projets pour les 5 prochaines années avec *might, will probably, I'm planning to, if…*.

### Corrigé

1. a) press / starts b) calls / 'll tell c) don't test / won't find
2. a) When I finish, I'll send it. b) If it's sunny, we'll go to the beach. c) Unless you study, you'll fail. (ou *If you don't study…*)
3. a) It might snow. b) She'll probably succeed. (ou *She's likely to pass.*) c) They probably won't come.
4. a) If you need help, call me. b) I'll be in a meeting at 3 p.m. c) If the project is approved, we'll start in January. d) I'm thinking of learning German.
5. Exemple : *In five years, I'll probably be a project manager. I might work abroad. If I pass my exams, I'll…*

## Module 11 — Le monde du travail (semaines 41 à 44)

**Objectifs** : te présenter dans un contexte pro, poser des questions polies, écrire un mail professionnel simple, utiliser les phrasal verbs les plus courants au travail.

### Leçon 1 : les questions indirectes (plus polies)

Une question indirecte commence par une formule polie, et **la suite reprend l'ordre d'une phrase normale** (sujet + verbe, sans *do / does / did*).

| Question directe | Question indirecte |
| --- | --- |
| Where is the meeting room? | Could you tell me where the meeting room **is**? |
| When does the project start? | Do you know when the project **starts**? |
| Did he send the report? | Do you know **if** he sent the report? |
| What time is it? | Could you tell me what time it **is**? |

Sans mot interrogatif, on ajoute **if** ou **whether** : *I'd like to know whether the price includes delivery.*

Formules d'introduction : *Could you tell me…? / Do you know…? / I was wondering… / Would you mind telling me…?*

### Leçon 2 : *make* ou *do* ?

| make (créer, produire) | do (activité, tâche) |
| --- | --- |
| make a decision | do your job |
| make a mistake | do a task |
| make a phone call | do business |
| make money | do research |
| make progress | do your best |
| make an appointment | do a favour |
| make a plan | do the shopping |
| make sure (s'assurer) | do overtime (heures sup) |

### Leçon 3 : les phrasal verbs du travail

| Phrasal verb | Sens | Exemple |
| --- | --- | --- |
| set up | mettre en place, installer | *We need to set up a meeting.* |
| carry out | réaliser, effectuer | *We carried out several tests.* |
| deal with | gérer, s'occuper de | *I'll deal with the client.* |
| follow up | faire le suivi, relancer | *I'm following up on my email.* |
| figure out | comprendre, trouver la solution | *We need to figure out the problem.* |
| look into | examiner, étudier | *I'll look into it.* |
| put off | reporter | *The meeting was put off until Friday.* |
| call off | annuler | *They called off the launch.* |
| come up with | proposer, trouver (une idée) | *She came up with a great solution.* |
| run out of | manquer de, être à court de | *We've run out of components.* |
| get back to | recontacter, revenir vers | *I'll get back to you tomorrow.* |
| take over | reprendre (en charge) | *He took over the project.* |

### Leçon 4 : l'entretien et le mail professionnel

**Se présenter en entretien**

- *I'm currently a robotics student and an apprentice project manager.*
- *I'm responsible for… / I'm in charge of… / I work on…*
- *My main strengths are… / I'm good at working in a team.*
- *I'm looking for a position where I can…*

**Structure d'un mail pro**

1. Salutation : *Dear Mr Smith, / Dear Sarah, / Hi Tom,* (selon le niveau de formalité)
2. Objet : *I'm writing to… / I'm writing regarding…*
3. Corps : *Please find attached… / Could you please…? / I would appreciate it if…*
4. Conclusion : *Let me know if you have any questions. / I look forward to hearing from you.*
5. Signature : *Best regards, / Kind regards, / Best,*

Exemple : *Dear Mr Brown, I'm writing regarding the delivery of the prototype. Please find attached the updated schedule. Could you please confirm the date by Friday? Let me know if you have any questions. Best regards, Erika Lajus*

### Pièges à éviter

- ✗ *Could you tell me where is the room?* → ✓ *Could you tell me where the room is?*
- ✗ *do a mistake* → ✓ *make a mistake*
- ✗ *I look forward to hear from you* → ✓ *I look forward to hearing from you* (*to* est ici une préposition)
- ✗ *Please find in attachment* → ✓ *Please find attached*

## Module 12 — Donner son avis (semaines 45 à 48)

**Objectifs** : exprimer et défendre une opinion, être d'accord ou non avec nuance, relier tes idées avec des propositions relatives.

### Leçon 1 : les pronoms relatifs

| Pronom | Pour | Exemple |
| --- | --- | --- |
| who | une personne | *The engineer who designed it is here.* |
| which | une chose | *The robot which we tested works well.* |
| that | personne ou chose (oral, phrases essentielles) | *The book that I read was great.* |
| where | un lieu | *The lab where I work is huge.* |
| whose | possession (dont le / la) | *The client whose order was late called.* |
| when | un moment | *I remember the day when we met.* |

On peut **supprimer** le relatif quand il est complément : *The robot (that) we tested.* Pas quand il est sujet : *The man who called* (obligatoire).

Avec des virgules (information en plus), jamais *that* : *My brother, who lives in London, is a doctor.*

### Leçon 2 : *too* et *enough*

| Structure | Sens | Exemple |
| --- | --- | --- |
| too + adjectif | trop | *It's too expensive.* |
| too much + indénombrable | trop de | *There's too much noise.* |
| too many + dénombrable | trop de | *There are too many bugs.* |
| adjectif + enough | assez | *It isn't big enough.* |
| enough + nom | assez de | *We don't have enough time.* |

Attention à la place : *enough* vient **après** l'adjectif mais **avant** le nom.

### Leçon 3 : exprimer son opinion

| Pour | Expressions |
| --- | --- |
| Donner son avis | *I think… / I believe… / In my opinion… / Personally, I feel that… / As far as I'm concerned…* |
| Être d'accord | *I agree. / That's true. / Exactly. / I couldn't agree more. / You have a point.* |
| Nuancer | *I partly agree. / That's true, but… / It depends. / I see what you mean, however…* |
| Ne pas être d'accord | *I don't agree. / I'm not sure about that. / I see it differently. / I'm afraid I disagree.* |
| Demander l'avis | *What do you think? / How do you feel about…? / What's your opinion on…?* |

Attention : *I agree* et jamais *I am agree*. *In my opinion* et jamais *For me* en début de phrase écrite (oral seulement).

### Leçon 4 : construire un argument

1. **Position** : *I believe that remote work is a good thing.*
2. **Raison 1 + exemple** : *Firstly, it saves time. For example, I don't spend an hour on the bus.*
3. **Raison 2** : *Secondly / Moreover, people are often more productive.*
4. **Concession** : *Of course, some people feel isolated. However…*
5. **Conclusion** : *To sum up / All in all, I think the advantages outweigh the disadvantages.*

### Pièges à éviter

- ✗ *The person which* → ✓ *The person who*
- ✗ *enough big* → ✓ *big enough*
- ✗ *I am agree with you* → ✓ *I agree with you*
- ✗ *My sister, that lives in Lyon* → ✓ *My sister, who lives in Lyon*

### Exercices

1. *Who, which, where* ou *whose* ? a) That's the woman \_\_\_ husband is a pilot. b) The city \_\_\_ I was born is small. c) The app \_\_\_ you recommended is great. d) The colleague \_\_\_ helped me is on holiday.
2. *Too* ou *enough* ? a) This box is \_\_\_ heavy to lift. b) He isn't old \_\_\_ to drive. c) There are \_\_\_ many people here. d) We have \_\_\_ chairs for everyone.
3. Réagis à : « Social media is bad for teenagers. » a) d'accord b) en partie d'accord c) pas d'accord
4. Traduis : a) Le projet sur lequel je travaille est passionnant. b) À mon avis, c'est trop cher. c) Je vois ce que tu veux dire, mais je ne suis pas d'accord. d) Nous n'avons pas assez de temps.
5. Écris un texte argumenté de 150 mots : « Faut-il interdire les voitures en centre-ville ? »

### Corrigé

1. a) whose b) where c) which (ou *that*) d) who
2. a) too b) enough c) too d) enough
3. Exemples : a) *I couldn't agree more, they spend too much time online.* b) *I partly agree, but it depends on how they use it.* c) *I see it differently: it helps them stay in touch.*
4. a) The project (that) I'm working on is exciting. b) In my opinion, it's too expensive. c) I see what you mean, but I don't agree. d) We don't have enough time.
5. Réponse libre. Vérifie la structure en 5 étapes de la leçon 4 et au moins 4 connecteurs.

## Module 13 — Bilan B1 et voix passive (semaines 49 à 52)

**Objectifs** : maîtriser les temps vus depuis le début, découvrir la voix passive simple et décrire comment un objet est fabriqué ou fonctionne.

### Leçon 1 : récapitulatif des temps

| Temps | Forme | Usage principal | Exemple |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Présent simple | I work | habitude, vérité | *I work in Bordeaux.* |
| Présent continu | I'm working | maintenant, temporaire | *I'm working on a new project.* |
| Prétérit | I worked | passé terminé et daté | *I worked late yesterday.* |
| Prétérit continu | I was working | décor dans le passé | *I was working when you called.* |
| Present perfect | I've worked | expérience, résultat, durée | *I've worked here for a year.* |
| Futur (will) | I'll work | décision, prédiction | *I'll work on it tomorrow.* |
| Futur (going to) | I'm going to work | projet décidé | *I'm going to work abroad.* |
| Used to | I used to work | habitude passée finie | *I used to work in a shop.* |

### Leçon 2 : la voix passive simple

La voix passive met en avant **l'objet** ou le **résultat**, pas la personne qui agit. Très utilisée en anglais technique.

**Forme** : *to be* (au bon temps) + **participe passé**

| Temps | Actif | Passif |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Présent | They make the parts in Germany. | The parts **are made** in Germany. |
| Prétérit | Someone built this robot in 2020. | This robot **was built** in 2020. |
| Present perfect | They have sent the order. | The order **has been sent**. |
| Futur | They will deliver it on Monday. | It **will be delivered** on Monday. |

Pour dire qui fait l'action : **by** : *The software was developed by our team.*

### Leçon 3 : décrire un objet et un processus

**Décrire un objet**

- *It's made of aluminium / plastic / steel.* (fabriqué en)
- *It's used for measuring temperature.* (sert à)
- *It consists of three parts: a base, an arm and a gripper.*
- *It weighs 2 kg. It's 30 cm long and 10 cm wide.*

**Décrire un processus** (passif + connecteurs)

*First, the components are ordered. Then, the board is assembled and soldered. After that, it is tested. Finally, it is packed and shipped to the client.*

### Leçon 4 : vocabulaire technique de base

- Matériaux : *steel, aluminium, plastic, copper, rubber, glass, wood*
- Outils : *screwdriver, wrench (spanner en GB), drill, soldering iron, pliers, multimeter*
- Machines et pièces : *motor, sensor, gear, belt, bearing, screw, bolt, nut, cable, battery*
- Verbes : *assemble, measure, cut, drill, weld, solder, connect, test, adjust, replace*

### Pièges à éviter

- ✗ *The robot was build* → ✓ *The robot was built* (participe passé)
- ✗ *It is made in aluminium* → ✓ *It is made of aluminium* (*made in* = le pays)
- ✗ *It serves to measure* → ✓ *It's used for measuring* / *It's used to measure*
- ✗ *The order has sent* → ✓ *The order has been sent*

### Exercices (test de fin de B1)

1. Conjugue au bon temps : a) I (live) here since 2021. b) Look! The machine (work) again. c) He (break) his arm when he (ski). d) I promise I (send) it tonight. e) When I was young, I (use to / hate) maths.
2. Mets au passif : a) They test every product. b) Someone stole my bike yesterday. c) The team has finished the prototype. d) They will announce the results next week.
3. Complète : a) The case is made \_\_\_ plastic. b) This tool is used \_\_\_ cutting wires. c) The app was designed \_\_\_ a small team.
4. Traduis : a) Les pièces sont fabriquées en France. b) Le moteur a été remplacé. c) Ce capteur sert à mesurer la distance. d) Le colis sera livré demain.
5. Décris un objet que tu utilises au travail (matière, taille, fonction, parties) en 8 phrases.
6. Explique en 6 étapes comment on fabrique une carte électronique, au passif.

### Corrigé

1. a) have lived b) is working c) broke / was skiing d) will send e) used to hate
2. a) Every product is tested. b) My bike was stolen yesterday. c) The prototype has been finished. d) The results will be announced next week.
3. a) of b) for c) by

5. Exemple : *A multimeter is a small electronic device. It's made of plastic. It's used for measuring voltage…*

## Module 14 — Le passé en détail (semaines 53 à 56)

**Objectifs** : expliquer l'historique d'un projet, l'ordre des événements passés et la durée d'une action jusqu'à maintenant, par exemple pour justifier un retard.

### Leçon 1 : le plus-que-parfait (had + participe passé)

Il indique qu'une action s'est produite **avant une autre action passée** (le « passé du passé »).

- *When I arrived, the meeting had already started.* (la réunion a commencé avant mon arrivée)
- *We had tested the system twice before the client noticed the bug.*
- *I didn't recognise him because he had changed a lot.*

Comparaison :

- *When I arrived, the train left.* (il part au moment où j'arrive)
- *When I arrived, the train had left.* (il était déjà parti)

Mots-repères : *already, before, after, by the time, because, never… before*

*By the time we found the problem, we had lost two weeks.* (le temps que…)

### Leçon 2 : le present perfect continu (have been + -ing)

Il insiste sur la **durée** ou l'**activité en cours** qui a commencé dans le passé et continue (ou vient de s'arrêter avec un résultat visible).

|  | Exemple |
| --- | --- |
| Affirmatif | I've been working on this project for six months. |
| Négatif | She hasn't been sleeping well lately. |
| Question | How long have you been waiting? |

Present perfect simple ou continu ?

| Simple : résultat, quantité | Continu : durée, activité |
| --- | --- |
| *I've written three reports.* | *I've been writing reports all morning.* |
| *I've fixed the bug.* (c'est fini) | *I've been fixing bugs.* (et je suis fatiguée) |

Résultat visible : *You're out of breath! — I've been running.*

### Leçon 3 : la concordance des temps dans un récit

Dans un récit au passé, on combine les 4 temps :

| Temps | Rôle | Exemple |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Prétérit | événements principaux | *The client called on Monday.* |
| Prétérit continu | décor, action en cours | *We were finishing the tests.* |
| Plus-que-parfait | ce qui s'était passé avant | *We had already found two issues.* |
| Plus-que-parfait continu | durée avant un moment passé | *We had been working on it for weeks.* |

### Leçon 4 : expliquer un retard de projet

- *The project has been delayed because a supplier had sent the wrong parts.*
- *We've been dealing with this issue since last week.*
- *By the time the parts arrived, we had already lost ten days.*
- *We had planned to deliver in March, but we've had to change the schedule.*
- *We've been doing everything we can to catch up.*

### Pièges à éviter

- ✗ *I am working here since 2 years* → ✓ *I've been working here for 2 years*
- ✗ *When I arrived, they already left* (sens flou) → ✓ *When I arrived, they had already left*
- ✗ *I've been knowing him for years* → ✓ *I've known him for years* (verbe d'état)
- ✗ *How long are you waiting?* → ✓ *How long have you been waiting?*

### Exercices

1. Prétérit ou plus-que-parfait ? a) When we got to the cinema, the film (start). b) She (be) tired because she (work) all night. c) I (never / see) the sea before I (go) to Brittany.
2. Present perfect simple ou continu ? a) I (read) this book for two weeks. b) I (read) three chapters. c) It (rain) all day. d) He (lose) his keys.
3. Complète avec *by the time*, *already* ou *before* : a) \_\_\_ I called, she had left. b) I had \_\_\_ eaten when they arrived. c) He had never flown \_\_\_ that trip.
4. Traduis : a) Je travaille sur ce projet depuis six mois. b) Quand le client a appelé, nous avions déjà corrigé l'erreur. c) Ça fait combien de temps que tu apprends l'anglais ? d) Nous avions prévu de livrer en mars.
5. Écris un mail de 120 mots à un client pour expliquer l'historique d'un retard (cause, ce qui avait été fait, ce que vous faites depuis).

### Corrigé

1. a) had started b) was / had been working (ou *had worked*) c) had never seen / went
2. a) have been reading b) have read c) has been raining d) has lost
3. a) By the time b) already c) before
4. a) I've been working on this project for six months. b) When the client called, we had already fixed the error. c) How long have you been learning English? d) We had planned to deliver in March.
5. Réponse libre. Vérifie : au moins un plus-que-parfait, un present perfect continu, et une formule d'excuse.

## Module 15 — Imaginer et regretter (semaines 57 à 60)

**Objectifs** : parler de situations imaginaires, de ce qui aurait pu se passer, exprimer un souhait ou un regret, discuter de scénarios et de décisions.

### Leçon 1 : le conditionnel 2 (situation imaginaire au présent)

**If + prétérit, would + verbe** : une situation irréelle ou peu probable.

- *If I had more time, I would learn Japanese.* (je n'ai pas le temps)
- *If I were you, I'd accept the offer.* (conseil)
- *What would you do if you won the lottery?*

Avec *to be*, on utilise **were** pour toutes les personnes (surtout à l'écrit) : *If I were rich… If he were here…*

Conditionnel 1 ou 2 ? *If it rains, I'll stay home.* (possible) / *If it snowed in July, I'd be surprised.* (improbable)

### Leçon 2 : le conditionnel 3 (irréel du passé)

**If + plus-que-parfait, would have + participe passé** : imaginer un passé différent.

- *If we had tested it earlier, we would have found the bug.* (on ne l'a pas fait)
- *If I had known, I would have called you.*
- *She wouldn't have missed the train if she had left on time.*

Autres modaux : *could have* (aurait pu), *might have* (aurait peut-être) : *We could have finished sooner.*

Conditionnel mixte (passé → présent) : *If I had studied engineering, I would be an engineer now.*

### Leçon 3 : *wish* et *if only* (souhaits et regrets)

| Structure | Sens | Exemple |
| --- | --- | --- |
| wish + prétérit | souhait sur le présent | *I wish I spoke Spanish.* (je ne le parle pas) |
| wish + plus-que-parfait | regret sur le passé | *I wish I had studied harder.* |
| wish + would | agacement, souhait de changement | *I wish he would stop talking.* |
| if only + … | même chose, plus fort | *If only I had listened!* |

Autres expressions : *I regret not + -ing* (*I regret not taking the job*), *I should have…* (*I should have checked the file*).

### Leçon 4 : discuter de scénarios et de décisions

- *What would happen if we changed the supplier?*
- *Suppose we had chosen option B, would it have been cheaper?*
- *In hindsight, we should have tested the prototype earlier.* (avec du recul)
- *If we hadn't reacted quickly, the client would have cancelled the order.*
- *Let's consider the worst-case scenario.* (le pire scénario)

### Pièges à éviter

- ✗ *If I would have time* → ✓ *If I had time* (jamais *would* après *if*)
- ✗ *If I would have known* → ✓ *If I had known*
- ✗ *I wish I can* → ✓ *I wish I could*
- ✗ *I would have do* → ✓ *I would have done*

### Exercices

1. Conditionnel 2 : a) If I (have) a car, I (drive) to work. b) If she (be) here, she (know) what to do. c) What (you / do) if you (lose) your job?
2. Conditionnel 3 : a) If we (leave) earlier, we (not / miss) the flight. b) If he (check) the cables, the robot (not / stop). c) I (help) you if you (ask) me.
3. Exprime un souhait ou un regret avec *wish* : a) Je ne sais pas nager. b) Je n'ai pas révisé. c) Il fait toujours du bruit.
4. Traduis : a) Si j'étais toi, je parlerais au manager. b) Si nous avions su, nous aurions changé le planning. c) J'aurais dû vérifier le fichier. d) Avec du recul, c'était la bonne décision.
5. Écris 120 mots : « Si tu pouvais changer une décision de ta vie, laquelle et pourquoi ? »

### Corrigé

1. a) had / would drive b) were / would know c) would you do / lost
2. a) had left / wouldn't have missed b) had checked / wouldn't have stopped c) would have helped / had asked
3. a) I wish I could swim. b) I wish I had studied. (ou *revised*) c) I wish he would stop making noise.
4. a) If I were you, I'd talk to the manager. b) If we had known, we would have changed the schedule. c) I should have checked the file. d) In hindsight, it was the right decision.
5. Réponse libre. Vérifie : au moins un conditionnel 3, un *wish* et un *should have*.

## Module 16 — Rapporter des paroles (semaines 61 à 64)

**Objectifs** : rapporter ce qu'une personne a dit ou demandé, faire le compte rendu d'une réunion, écrire un mail de suivi clair.

### Leçon 1 : le discours indirect, les temps reculent

Quand le verbe d'introduction est au passé (*said, told*), le temps de la phrase rapportée **recule d'un cran**.

| Paroles directes | Discours indirect |
| --- | --- |
| « I **work** in Lyon. » | She said (that) she **worked** in Lyon. |
| « I **am working** on it. » | He said he **was working** on it. |
| « We **finished** yesterday. » | They said they **had finished** the day before. |
| « I\*\*'ve sent\*\* the file. » | She said she **had sent** the file. |
| « I **will call** you. » | He said he **would call** me. |
| « I **can** come. » | She said she **could** come. |
| « You **must** sign. » | He said I **had to** sign. |

Les repères changent aussi : *today → that day, tomorrow → the next day / the following day, yesterday → the day before, here → there, now → then, this → that, next week → the following week.*

Pas de recul si la phrase est toujours vraie ou si le verbe d'introduction est au présent : *He says he's tired. She said the Earth is round.*

### Leçon 2 : *say* ou *tell* ?

- **say** (something) : *She said that the price was too high.* / *He said hello.*
- **tell** + **quelqu'un** : *She told me that the price was too high.* / *Tell him the truth.*

✗ *She said me* → ✓ *She told me* ou *She said to me*.

### Leçon 3 : rapporter des questions et des ordres

**Questions** : ordre sujet + verbe, sans *do*, avec *if / whether* pour les questions fermées.

- « Where **do you live**? » → *He asked me where **I lived**.*
- « **Are you** free on Monday? » → *She asked **if I was** free on Monday.*

**Ordres et demandes** : *tell / ask + quelqu'un + to + verbe*

- « Send me the report. » → *She told me **to send** her the report.*
- « Please don't be late. » → *He asked us **not to be** late.*

### Leçon 4 : les verbes rapporteurs

| Verbe | Construction | Exemple |
| --- | --- | --- |
| suggest | + -ing / + that | *She suggested postponing the meeting.* |
| recommend | + -ing / + that | *He recommended using a new supplier.* |
| advise | + quelqu'un + to | *They advised us to wait.* |
| warn | + quelqu'un + (not) to / about | *He warned me not to touch the cable.* |
| promise | + to | *She promised to send it today.* |
| agree | + to / + that | *The client agreed to extend the deadline.* |
| refuse | + to | *They refused to pay the extra cost.* |
| explain | + that | *He explained that the parts were out of stock.* |
| remind | + quelqu'un + to | *Can you remind me to call him?* |
| admit | + -ing / + that | *He admitted making a mistake.* |

Attention : ✗ *suggest to do* → ✓ *suggest doing* / *suggest that we do*.

### Leçon 5 : le compte rendu de réunion

*Meeting summary — Project X — 20 October*

- *Attendees: …*
- *The client explained that the first prototype was too heavy.*
- *Erika suggested using aluminium instead of steel.*
- *The client agreed to review the new design next week.*
- *Action items: Erika to send the updated drawings by Friday. Tom to check the budget.*
- *Next meeting: 27 October.*

### Pièges à éviter

- ✗ *He said me that* → ✓ *He told me that*
- ✗ *She asked me where did I live* → ✓ *She asked me where I lived*
- ✗ *He suggested to wait* → ✓ *He suggested waiting*
- ✗ *He told to send it* → ✓ *He told me to send it*

### Exercices

2. *Say* ou *tell* ? a) He \_\_\_ me the news. b) She \_\_\_ that she was ready. c) Can you \_\_\_ him to call me? d) What did they \_\_\_?

## Module 17 — Anglais technique (semaines 65 à 68)

**Objectifs** : présenter un prototype ou un système, décrire un problème technique et sa cause probable, lire et écrire une spécification simple.

### Leçon 1 : la voix passive à tous les temps

| Temps | Passif | Exemple |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Présent simple | is / are + PP | *The data is stored on the SD card.* |
| Présent continu | is / are being + PP | *The firmware is being updated.* |
| Prétérit | was / were + PP | *The board was redesigned last month.* |
| Present perfect | has / have been + PP | *All the units have been tested.* |
| Plus-que-parfait | had been + PP | *The sensor had been damaged during shipping.* |
| Futur | will be + PP | *The software will be released in June.* |
| Modal | can / must / should be + PP | *The battery must be charged before use.* |

En spécification, *shall* exprime une exigence : *The system shall stop within 0.5 seconds.*

### Leçon 2 : *have something done* (faire faire)

**have / get + objet + participe passé** : quelqu'un d'autre fait l'action pour toi.

- *We had the PCBs manufactured in Shenzhen.* (on les a fait fabriquer)
- *I'm getting my laptop repaired.*
- *You should have the motor checked by a technician.*

### Leçon 3 : les modaux de déduction (diagnostiquer)

| Certitude | Présent | Passé |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Presque sûr que oui | *It must be the power supply.* | *It must have overheated.* |
| Possible | *It might / could / may be a loose cable.* | *It might have been damaged.* |
| Presque sûr que non | *It can't be the software.* | *It can't have been the battery, it's new.* |

Exemple de diagnostic : *The robot stopped suddenly. It can't be the battery because it was fully charged. It might be the motor driver. Actually, the fuse has blown, so there must have been a short circuit.*

### Leçon 4 : présenter un prototype

1. **Introduction** : *Today, I'd like to present our new prototype, a mobile robot for warehouse logistics.*
2. **Fonction** : *It's designed to carry loads up to 50 kg autonomously.*
3. **Composants** : *It consists of a chassis, two DC motors, a lidar sensor and an embedded controller.*
4. **Fonctionnement** : *The lidar scans the environment and the controller calculates the path.*
5. **Performances** : *It can reach 1.5 m/s and the battery lasts up to 8 hours.*
6. **Prochaines étapes** : *The next step is to test it on site.*
7. **Questions** : *I'm happy to answer any questions.*

### Leçon 5 : décrire un problème et sa résolution

- *We've noticed an issue with… / The system keeps crashing when…*
- *The root cause seems to be… / After investigation, we found that…*
- *As a workaround, we… / A fix will be deployed by…*
- *To prevent this from happening again, we will…*

Lire les nombres : *0.5* = « zero point five » (point, pas virgule), *1,500* = mille cinq cents (la virgule sépare les milliers), *±2 mm* = « plus or minus two millimetres ».

### Pièges à éviter

- ✗ *The tests have been make* → ✓ *The tests have been made*
- ✗ *0,5 V* → ✓ *0.5 V* (point décimal en anglais)
- ✗ *It must be broke* → ✓ *It must be broken* / *It must have broken*
- ✗ *I made repair my car* → ✓ *I had my car repaired*

### Exercices

1. Mets au passif : a) We are testing the new version. b) They had already shipped the parts. c) You must calibrate the sensor every month. d) Someone will install the software tomorrow.
2. Utilise *have something done* : a) Un technicien répare la machine pour nous. b) J'ai fait imprimer le rapport. c) Nous allons faire contrôler les freins.
3. Fais une déduction : a) The screen is black and the LED is off. (power?) b) It worked yesterday and nobody touched it. (software update?) c) The cable is brand new. (cable? → non)
4. Traduis : a) Le système doit s'arrêter en moins d'une seconde. b) La carte a été reconçue le mois dernier. c) Ça doit être un faux contact. d) Le correctif sera déployé vendredi.
5. Prépare une présentation de 150 mots d'un projet technique que tu connais, avec les 7 étapes de la leçon 4.
6. Écris un rapport d'incident de 100 mots (problème, cause, solution provisoire, solution définitive).

### Corrigé

2. a) We have the machine repaired by a technician. b) I had the report printed. c) We're going to have the brakes checked.
3. a) It must be a power problem. b) It might have been the software update. c) It can't be the cable.
4. a) The system shall stop within one second. (ou *must stop in less than one second*) b) The board was redesigned last month. c) It must be a loose connection. d) The fix will be deployed on Friday.
5. Exemple : *Today, I'd like to present our new device, a connected sensor designed to detect falls…*
6. Réponse libre. Vérifie : *root cause*, *workaround*, *fix* et le passif.

## Module 18 — Négocier et convaincre (semaines 69 à 72)

**Objectifs** : négocier un délai, un budget ou un périmètre avec un client, répondre à une objection, articuler un raisonnement avec des connecteurs logiques précis.

### Leçon 1 : les connecteurs logiques

| Fonction | Connecteurs | Exemple |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opposition | however, whereas, while, on the other hand | *Option A is cheaper, whereas option B is faster.* |
| Concession | although, even though, despite, in spite of | *Although the price is high, the quality is excellent.* |
| Cause | because, since, as, due to, owing to | *The delivery is late due to a shortage of chips.* |
| Conséquence | therefore, so, as a result, consequently | *The test failed. Therefore, we need more time.* |
| Ajout | moreover, furthermore, in addition, besides | *Moreover, the new design is lighter.* |
| But | in order to, so as to, so that | *We added a sensor in order to improve safety.* |
| Condition | provided that, as long as, unless | *We can deliver in May provided that you confirm today.* |

Construction : *despite / in spite of / due to* + **nom ou -ing** (*despite the delay, despite being late*) ; *although / even though / because* + **phrase complète** (*although it was late*).

### Leçon 2 : l'inversion d'insistance

Après certaines expressions négatives en début de phrase, on inverse sujet et auxiliaire, comme dans une question. Style formel et persuasif.

- *Not only does it reduce costs, but it also improves safety.*
- *Never have we seen such good results.*
- *Only after the tests will we be able to confirm.*
- *Under no circumstances should the machine be opened during operation.*

### Leçon 3 : les phrases de négociation

| Étape | Expressions |
| --- | --- |
| Ouvrir | *Thank you for your time. Let's go through the main points.* |
| Proposer | *We'd like to propose… / What if we… ? / How about… ?* |
| Conditionner | *We could agree to that, provided that… / If you can…, we can…* |
| Résister poliment | *I'm afraid that won't be possible. / That's a bit more than we expected.* |
| Faire une concession | *We could consider… / We'd be willing to…* |
| Demander une contrepartie | *In return, we would need… / That would only work if…* |
| Conclure | *So, to sum up, we've agreed on… / Let's put that in writing.* |

Le conditionnel et *I'm afraid* adoucissent un refus : *I'm afraid we can't lower the price, but we could include training.*

### Leçon 4 : répondre à une objection

Méthode **écouter → reformuler → répondre → vérifier** :

1. *I understand your concern.*
2. *If I understand correctly, you're worried about the maintenance cost.*
3. *Actually, the new model requires 30 % less maintenance, which means lower costs in the long term.*
4. *Does that answer your question?*

Exemple de négociation de délai : *I'm afraid we won't be able to deliver by the end of March due to a supplier issue. However, we could deliver a first version on 15 April, provided that the specifications are frozen this week. In return, we would extend the warranty by six months.*

### Pièges à éviter

- ✗ *Despite it was late* → ✓ *Although it was late* / *Despite the delay*
- ✗ *Not only it reduces costs* → ✓ *Not only does it reduce costs*
- ✗ *We can't. Point.* (trop direct) → ✓ *I'm afraid that won't be possible.*
- ✗ *Eventually, we can give a discount* (= finalement) → ✓ *Possibly, we can give a discount*

### Exercices

1. Choisis : a) \_\_\_ the bad weather, the test was a success. (Although / Despite) b) The project is late \_\_\_ a lack of resources. (due to / because) c) We'll start on Monday \_\_\_ you sign the contract. (provided that / despite) d) It's expensive. \_\_\_, it's very reliable. (However / Therefore)
2. Réécris avec une inversion : a) We have never had such a big order. (Never…) b) It saves time and it also saves money. (Not only…)
3. Rends ces phrases plus diplomatiques : a) No, we can't do that. b) Your price is too high. c) You must pay before delivery.
4. Traduis : a) Bien que le délai soit court, nous ferons de notre mieux. b) Nous pourrions accepter, à condition que vous confirmiez aujourd'hui. c) En contrepartie, nous aurions besoin d'un acompte. d) Je comprends votre inquiétude.
5. Écris un dialogue de négociation (12 répliques) : le client veut une livraison 2 semaines plus tôt, tu proposes une solution avec une contrepartie.

### Corrigé

1. a) Despite b) due to c) provided that d) However
2. a) Never have we had such a big order. b) Not only does it save time, but it also saves money.
3. a) I'm afraid that won't be possible. b) That's a bit higher than we expected. c) We would need payment before delivery.
4. a) Although the deadline is short, we'll do our best. b) We could accept, provided that you confirm today. c) In return, we would need a deposit. d) I understand your concern.
5. Réponse libre. Vérifie : une condition (*provided that*), une concession, une contrepartie et une conclusion.

### Vocabulaire du module

## Module 19 — Nuances et naturel (semaines 73 à 76)

**Objectifs** : parler de façon plus naturelle, comprendre l'implicite et l'humour, faire du small talk professionnel, choisir entre gérondif et infinitif.

### Leçon 1 : gérondif (-ing) ou infinitif (to) ?

| Verbe + -ing | Verbe + to | Les deux, avec un changement de sens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| enjoy, avoid, finish, mind, suggest, keep, consider, risk, miss, deny, can't help | want, need, decide, hope, plan, promise, refuse, agree, manage, afford, seem, offer | stop, remember, forget, try, regret |
| *I enjoy working with robots.* | *We decided to change the design.* | voir ci-dessous |

Changement de sens :

- *I stopped smoking.* (j'ai arrêté de fumer) / *I stopped to smoke.* (je me suis arrêté pour fumer)
- *I remember meeting him.* (je me souviens de l'avoir rencontré) / *Remember to call him.* (pense à l'appeler)
- *Try restarting it.* (essaie, pour voir) / *I tried to open it, but I couldn't.* (j'ai fait l'effort)

Après une **préposition**, toujours -ing : *I'm interested in learning. Thank you for helping. Before leaving…*

### Leçon 5 : le small talk et l'implicite britannique

**Small talk en début de réunion** : *How was your weekend? / Did you have a good trip? / Busy week? / Lovely weather today, isn't it?*

| Ce qui est dit | Ce que ça veut souvent dire |
| --- | --- |
| *That's an interesting idea.* | Je ne suis pas convaincu. |
| *I'll bear it in mind.* | Je ne vais probablement rien en faire. |
| *Not bad.* | C'est bien, voire très bien. |
| *With all due respect…* | Je ne suis pas du tout d'accord. |
| *Could we possibly…?* | Il faut le faire. |

### Pièges à éviter

- ✗ *I look forward to see you* → ✓ *I look forward to seeing you*
- ✗ *I enjoy to work* → ✓ *I enjoy working*
- ✗ *I am used to work late* → ✓ *I am used to working late*
- ✗ *The more it is easy* → ✓ *The easier it is*
